The solution

One phone call, from first hello to portable work history

A sector-agnostic, phone-only flow. Workers just listen and speak in their own language.

HAAZRI_NET
MenuStart

Model 1100 voice OS. Simulation: tap Start, then follow the screen.

  1. Register

    The worker dials in. Voice onboarding captures name, location and skills in their own language, powered by Bhashini.

  2. Skill verify

    Skill tier is classified as skilled, semi-skilled or unskilled from natural speech. No forms, no typing.

  3. Match

    Verified jobs are matched on wage floor, location and tier, then sent as a short SMS. Press 1 to apply.

  4. Wage setup

    Workers set a wage floor or switch on urgent-work mode. No more vague, verbal rates.

  5. Night-before alert

    IVR and SMS confirm site address, wage and tools the evening before, so no-shows drop.

  6. Work history

    Every finished job adds to a portable digital record that feeds back into matching and builds trust over time.

Why it works

Built for the feature phone in the worker's pocket

Voice and keypad only

Callers hear prompts and press keys or speak. No smartphone, data plan, app or literacy needed.

Their own language

Hindi, Kannada and English, using Bhashini speech recognition and text-to-speech.

Missed call to start

A missed call costs the worker nothing. HAAZRI calls back and walks them through.
