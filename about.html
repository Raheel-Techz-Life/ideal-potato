About us

The trust layer for India's invisible workforce

HAAZRI is an initiative by CogniStruct Pvt. Ltd., built on ONEST and Bhashini and piloting in Hubballi-Dharwad.

Why we exist

Hiring is an exhausting, continuous crisis

The employer's struggle

  • No verified skill proof. Masons are hired blind. Incompetence shows up mid-job as rework and wasted material.
  • Last-minute no-shows. One missing loader can halt twenty. There is no backup pipeline.
  • No tiered pools. Every project means rebuilding the skilled and unskilled mix from scratch.
  • Zero worker loyalty. Daily wages work against a reliable, returning crew.

The worker's struggle

  • Skilled workers stay invisible. Years of experience, but reputation doesn't travel beyond a few contractors.
  • Semi-skilled workers get misclassified. Mixer operators are paid as unskilled with no way to prove their impact.
  • Unskilled workers face wage games. Loaders and helpers have no transparency and no path up.
  • Income is a gamble. People leave home at 5:00 AM without knowing if the site is active or the wage is real.

Hiring is an exhausting, continuous crisis. Without a backup pipeline, project timelines collapse.

Shivanand, contractor, Hubballi-Dharwad field research 2024–25
Roadmap

Pilot, scale, expand

  1. Months 0–6

    Pilot

    Validate fill rates in Hubballi's Smart City corridor, where 59 of 63 projects are complete and private demand is rising.

  2. Months 6–18

    Scale

    Expand across Karnataka. Launch the first enterprise API integrations and B2G pilots with labour departments.

  3. Months 18–36

    Expand

    Go national, with embedded financial services, micro-insurance and credit scoring for workers.
