How it works

Three steps, all on a normal phone

1

Worker calls

A missed call starts it. Voice prompts capture name, trade and location in Hindi, Kannada or English.

2

HAAZRI verifies and matches

Skill tier and past jobs are checked, then matched to open jobs and sent as a short SMS.

3

Employer gets a candidate

A verified, voice-screened candidate within 24 hours. If not, your next 5 hires are on us.
